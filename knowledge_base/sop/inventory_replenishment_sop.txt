INVENTORY REPLENISHMENT SOP

Purpose:

Define the standard process for identifying inventory that requires
replenishment.

Inventory Monitoring:

Inventory should be monitored using current stock and reorder point.

Low Stock Condition:

An item is considered low stock when current inventory is less than or
equal to the defined reorder point.

Replenishment Investigation:

When low stock is detected:

1. Identify the product SKU.
2. Identify the warehouse.
3. Check current inventory.
4. Check reorder point.
5. Review related supplier information.
6. Review open shipments when available.

Recommended Action:

The operations team should evaluate replenishment requirements based on
current demand, inventory levels, supplier availability, and expected
delivery timelines.

Human approval should be obtained before executing material procurement
actions.
