STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
DELAYED CUSTOMER ORDER

Purpose:

Provide a standard procedure for investigating customer orders that are
potentially delayed.

Step 1: Identify Order

Retrieve the order using the order number.

Verify:

- Customer
- Product SKU
- Quantity
- Order status

Step 2: Check Shipment

Identify the shipment associated with the order.

Verify:

- Shipment number
- Shipment status
- Expected delivery date
- Supplier

Step 3: Check Supplier

Retrieve supplier information and review supplier reliability.

Step 4: Check Inventory

Verify current inventory for the ordered product SKU.

Compare current stock against the reorder point.

Step 5: Determine Root Cause

Potential causes include:

- Supplier shipment delay
- Insufficient inventory
- Warehouse availability issue
- Operational processing delay

Step 6: Recommend Action

The system should provide a recommendation based on verified operational
data and relevant supply-chain policies.

Human approval may be required before executing operational actions.
