SUPPLIER ESCALATION POLICY

Purpose:

Define when supplier-related operational issues should be escalated.

Escalation Conditions:

Supplier issues may require escalation when:

- Shipments remain delayed.
- Supplier reliability is consistently low.
- Customer orders are materially affected.
- Supplier does not provide an updated delivery commitment.
- Repeated operational incidents occur.

Escalation Process:

1. Verify the operational issue.
2. Collect relevant shipment and supplier information.
3. Contact the supplier.
4. Record the supplier response.
5. Escalate unresolved or repeated issues to supplier management.

Important:

Escalation decisions should be based on verified operational data and
applicable company policies.
