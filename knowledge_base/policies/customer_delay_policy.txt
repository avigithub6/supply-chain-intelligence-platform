CUSTOMER ORDER DELAY POLICY

Purpose:

Define the operational approach for customer orders affected by supply
chain delays.

Delay Verification:

Before communicating a delay, operations personnel should verify the
order and associated shipment information.

Required Information:

- Order number
- Customer
- Product SKU
- Quantity
- Shipment status
- Expected delivery date

Customer Communication:

Customer communication should be based on verified operational information.

The system should not claim a delivery date unless a reliable delivery
estimate is available.

Recommended Communication:

When a delay is confirmed, provide:

1. Current order status.
2. Reason for the delay when verified.
3. Updated expected delivery information when available.
4. Appropriate next steps.

Accuracy:

Customer-facing responses should avoid unsupported assumptions.
