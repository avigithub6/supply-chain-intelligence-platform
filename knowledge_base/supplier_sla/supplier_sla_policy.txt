SUPPLIER SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT POLICY

Document Type:
Supplier SLA

Purpose:
This document defines the standard service level expectations for suppliers
participating in the supply chain.

Delivery Performance:

Suppliers are expected to deliver shipments within the agreed delivery
date specified in the purchase or shipment record.

A shipment is considered delayed when the actual or current delivery date
exceeds the expected delivery date.

Supplier Reliability:

Supplier reliability is evaluated using historical delivery performance,
shipment delays, fulfillment consistency, and operational incidents.

Suppliers with consistently low reliability scores should receive additional
monitoring.

Delay Escalation:

If a supplier shipment becomes delayed, the operations team should first
verify the shipment status and expected delivery date.

If the delay exceeds the acceptable operational threshold, the supplier
should be contacted for an updated delivery commitment.

Repeated supplier delays should be escalated to the supplier management team.

Recommended Actions:

1. Verify shipment status.
2. Check expected delivery date.
3. Review supplier reliability.
4. Contact supplier for updated delivery commitment.
5. Escalate repeated or severe delays.
