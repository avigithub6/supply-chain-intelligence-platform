SUPPLIER DELAY MANAGEMENT POLICY

Purpose:
Define the operational process for handling supplier-related shipment delays.

Initial Investigation:

When a shipment is delayed, operations personnel must verify:

- Shipment number
- Order number
- Supplier code
- Shipment status
- Expected delivery date

Supplier Assessment:

After confirming the shipment delay, the supplier's historical reliability
should be reviewed.

A low reliability score may indicate an increased risk of repeated delays.

Operational Response:

For an active delay:

1. Contact the supplier.
2. Request an updated estimated delivery date.
3. Record the supplier response.
4. Assess customer impact.
5. Escalate when required.

Customer Impact:

If a supplier delay affects a customer order, the operations team should
evaluate the expected customer delivery impact before taking action.

Escalation:

Repeated supplier delays should be escalated to the responsible supplier
management team.
